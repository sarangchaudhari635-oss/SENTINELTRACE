Deterministic SIEM Design Principles
Time-Window Determinism
Events are evaluated against rolling timestamps (occurred_at BETWEEN [event_time - window] AND event_time). Out-of-window events are excluded deterministically.
Fingerprint Deduplication
Matching rules generate a deterministic composite key. Existing open or acknowledged incidents are updated with latest timestamps and evidence count instead of spawning duplicate alarms.
Complete Audit Trail
Every incident lifecycle event (Creation, Acknowledged, Resolved) is recorded with immutable previous/new status values and timestamps in incident_audit_logs.
